Your webcam can see your heartbeat.

quiver is a motion microscope. It amplifies the invisible colour and motion changes in live video ×60 in real time — blood pulsing through your face, a chest breathing across the room, a table trembling under a laptop fan.

Nothing leaves your device. Best in even light, holding still for ~8 s. Not a medical device.
